V POWER 飛輪功率儀錶板

隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 12 日

V Power 是一款飛輪車儀錶板應用程式。本程式不會建立帳號、不會蒐集個人身分資料、 不含廣告或分析工具，也不會將任何資料傳送給開發者或第三方。

一、本程式處理哪些資料

運動與健康資料

連接藍牙感測器後，本程式會即時讀取踏頻、功率、心率，並據此計算阻力、 時間、平均功率與消耗熱量。這些數值僅保留在手機記憶體中， 結束騎乘後即消失，不會寫入任何資料庫或檔案。

裝置與網路資料

  • 附近藍牙裝置的名稱與訊號強度，僅用於顯示掃描清單並連接您指定的曲柄。
  • 車台識別名稱（Station ID）與配對的曲柄名稱，儲存在手機本機設定中， 供您下次開啟時沿用。

不會處理的資料

本程式不蒐集地理位置。Android 10 以下的系統版本規定， 藍牙掃描必須一併宣告位置權限，本程式因此保留該權限 （android:maxSdkVersion="30"），但從未讀取或記錄您的位置。 本程式也不存取通訊錄、相簿、檔案、麥克風或相機。

二、資料的傳輸

當您手動開啟「資料廣播」功能時，本程式會以 UDP 將目前的踏頻、功率、 心率、阻力與車台識別名稱，送往同一個區域網路（Wi-Fi）內由課程營運方 架設的計分主機，用於團體課程的即時排名與分組。

  • 此傳輸僅限區域網路，不會經由網際網路傳出，開發者無法接收或讀取。
  • 此功能預設為關閉，可隨時在畫面上開啟或關閉。關閉後即停止傳送。
  • 計分主機如何保存這些資料，由該場館的營運方負責，不在本政策範圍內。 請洽場館方瞭解其處理方式。

除上述情形外，本程式不會將任何資料傳送到手機以外的地方。 本程式不販售資料、不做廣告投放、不做使用行為分析。

三、權限說明

  • 附近的裝置／藍牙：尋找並連接飛輪車曲柄、心率帶等感測器。
  • 位置（Android 11 以下）：系統規定藍牙掃描須具備此權限，本程式不使用位置資訊。
  • 網路連線：僅用於前述區域網路資料廣播。

四、兒童

本程式非為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意蒐集兒童資料。

五、您的控制權

所有資料都在您的手機上。關閉資料廣播即停止對外傳送； 移除本程式即會一併刪除車台識別名稱與配對設定。

六、政策異動與聯絡方式

本政策如有修訂，將更新本頁並標示日期。 有任何疑問請來信：albert321liu@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

V Power is an indoor-cycling dashboard. It has no accounts, collects no personal information, contains no ads or analytics, and sends nothing to the developer or to any third party.

1. What the app handles

Fitness and health data

While connected to Bluetooth sensors, the app reads cadence, power and heart rate in real time and derives resistance, elapsed time, average power and calories. These values live in memory only and are gone when the ride ends — nothing is written to a database or a file.

Device and network data

  • The names and signal strengths of nearby Bluetooth devices, used only to show the scan list and connect to the crank you choose.
  • Your station ID and paired crank name, saved in the phone's local app settings so they persist between sessions.

What it does not handle

The app does not collect location. Android 10 and earlier require the location permission for any Bluetooth scan, so the app declares it with android:maxSdkVersion="30", but never reads or records your position. It does not access contacts, photos, files, the microphone or the camera.

2. Data transmission

When you switch on Data Broadcast yourself, the app sends the current cadence, power, heart rate, resistance and station ID over UDP to a scoring host on the same local Wi-Fi network, operated by your gym or class organiser, for live ranking and team assignment.

  • This traffic stays on the local network. It does not travel over the internet, and the developer cannot receive or read it.
  • The feature is off by default and can be switched off at any time from the dashboard.
  • How the scoring host retains that data is the responsibility of the venue operating it and is outside this policy. Please ask the venue about their practices.

Apart from the above, nothing leaves your phone. No data is sold, used for advertising, or used for behavioural analytics.

3. Permissions

  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth — to find and connect cranks, trainers and heart-rate straps.
  • Location (Android 11 and below) — required by the system for Bluetooth scanning; location itself is never used.
  • Network access — solely for the local-network broadcast described above.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

5. Your control

All data is on your device. Turning off Data Broadcast stops all outbound traffic; uninstalling the app removes the station ID and pairing settings with it.
